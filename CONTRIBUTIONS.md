# Contribution log

**Project:** Irreversibility Blindness: do LLM agents know what cannot be undone?
**Author:** Anirudh Vuppala (SE23UCSE007), Mahindra University. Solo project.

## Who did what

I did this project on my own. I chose the research questions, designed the sandbox testbed and the ScienceWorld slice, ran all experiments on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, labelled the human-check sample (and re-labelled it later for the retest), and checked every number in the report against the saved results.

## Use of AI assistance

I used Claude (Anthropic) as an assistant for coding, analysis scripts, LaTeX formatting, slide and report wording, and viva preparation. I reviewed, ran and tested what it produced, and I take responsibility for the code, the results and the claims in the report. The reported numbers come from the saved results in the repository (`results/analysis/final/headline_numbers.json`), not from the assistant's memory. The bibliography entries were checked against their sources.
